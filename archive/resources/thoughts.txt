Rules, Skills, and Hooks solve the Action Problem. They give the AI the capability to reliably and safely execute workflows, use tools, and make decisions without going off the rails. This architecture treats the AI not as a chatbot, but as an execution engine. Instead of just giving the AI a prompt, you wrap it in an infrastructure of control layers.
Rules (The Permanent Constraints)
Skills (The "How-To" Methodology)
Hooks (The Safety Triggers) - Hooks sit entirely outside the AI's reasoning loop. They are event-based triggers that enforce guarantees, similar to pre-commit hooks in software development.

RAG enhances LLM responses by retrieving relevant information at query time.
RAG answers the question: "How do I give the model access to information it wasn't trained on?"

RAG: Provides information the model needs but doesn't have. Skills: Provides capabilities the model needs but doesn't have.
Information vs. capability. Knowledge vs. action. That's the fundamental distinction.
